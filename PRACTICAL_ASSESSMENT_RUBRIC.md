# Practical PTW Assessment Rubric

Use a simple, realistic task with limited complexity. The assessment is nevertheless evidence of competency and must be invigilated.

## Mandatory gates

A learner is **Not Yet Competent** if any of these occur:

- Scope or location is materially unclear.
- A critical hazard is missed.
- Required isolation is omitted or treated as assumed.
- A required supporting certificate/checklist is omitted.
- Work is allowed to continue despite a missing critical control.
- The learner cannot explain stop-work and change-management expectations.
- The learner cannot explain safe handback and closure.

## Scored sections (100 points)

- Scope, location, equipment and boundaries: 15
- Work-type and supporting-document selection: 10
- Hazard identification: 15
- Controls and safeguards: 20
- Isolation and verification: 10
- PPE, tools and access controls: 5
- SIMOPS and area coordination: 5
- Toolbox Talk / communication: 10
- Revalidation, suspension and change: 5
- Handback and closure: 5

Recommended practical pass score: 80%, with all mandatory gates satisfied.

## Suggested simple scenarios

1. Replace a damaged non-process water-line support using hand tools and a portable drill in an operating utility area.
2. Install a small sign bracket at 2.2 m using a mobile platform in a pedestrian area.
3. Lift and reposition a small pump motor with a certified chain block inside a barricaded workshop bay.
4. Excavate a shallow trial pit after underground-service verification.

Each scenario must be checked against the approved site procedure before use. Avoid using a scenario whose hazards exceed the learner's intended authorization level.
