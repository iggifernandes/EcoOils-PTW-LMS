# Impact-video integration plan

Use short, facilitated clips, not passive viewing. Before each clip ask learners what control should have prevented the event. After the clip, require them to connect the lesson to a specific PTW field or authorization decision.

Recommended public sources for review and approval:

- U.S. Chemical Safety Board, "Dangers of Hot Work"
- U.S. Chemical Safety Board, "Hot Work: Hidden Hazards"
- IOGP Life-Saving Rules materials, especially Work Authorisation, Energy Isolation, Hot Work, Confined Space, Lifting and Working at Height

Do not embed third-party videos until licensing, availability, language and organizational suitability are checked. Prefer links or approved embedded players and provide captions/transcripts in English, Bahasa Indonesia and Bahasa Melayu.
